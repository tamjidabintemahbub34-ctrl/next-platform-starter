# Day 1 — Bangladesh Agency Lead Generation

**Project type:** Practice sample  
**Service demonstrated:** Web research, lead generation, data entry, source checking, and basic ICP matching.

## Project overview
This sample demonstrates a structured research workflow for identifying Bangladesh-based digital marketing agencies and organizing publicly available business contact information into a lead tracker.

## Scope
- 10 agency records collected
- Public business contact details researched
- Official websites and source references recorded in the working spreadsheet
- Verification status and follow-up needs tracked
- One record marked for manual follow-up where direct Bangladesh contact details were not confirmed

## Workflow
1. Define the target business profile (ICP).
2. Search for relevant agencies.
3. Prefer official company websites and directories as sources.
4. Record business details and source references.
5. Mark verification status; do not invent missing information.
6. Review records for duplicates, freshness, and completeness.

## Tools and AI use
AI-assisted research and organization were used to support discovery and drafting. Sources and contact details require human review before outreach or client delivery. A listed public email is source-checked, not proof that the mailbox is active.
