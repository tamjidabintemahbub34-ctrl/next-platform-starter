# Bangladesh Digital Marketing Agency Lead Research

![Day 1 Lead Generation portfolio cover](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/e31d82_104de8d62c424571b4ecae9d1d41322a~mv2.png)

**Project type:** Practice Sample (not paid client work)  
**Service demonstrated:** Web Research, Lead Generation, Data Entry, Source Verification  
**Market:** Bangladesh  
**Dataset:** 10 digital marketing agency records

## Project objective
Build a structured prospect list of Bangladesh-based digital marketing agencies and document where each public business contact detail was found.

## Work completed
- Collected 10 agency records in a structured Excel workbook.
- Recorded business name, services/industry, official website, public business email and phone where available, location, source URL, source type, and research notes.
- Added source and quality-check sheets to make review easier.
- Flagged records that need manual follow-up rather than guessing missing details.

## Quality and limitations
- The workbook contains 10 records, 9 public email entries, and 8 public phone entries.
- One record is marked for manual follow-up because the direct Bangladesh contact details were not confirmed.
- A listed public email is source-checked, not proof that the mailbox is active or that the person has consented to marketing.
- Some details sourced from company PDFs may need freshness checks.
- AI assisted discovery, organization, and drafting. The sources and entries must be manually rechecked before client delivery.

## Deliverables
The original Excel workbook is maintained by the portfolio owner. A public preview should use anonymized/sample rows where appropriate and should not expose a bulk list of personal contact details.

## Tools
Google Search, AI-assisted research, Microsoft Excel.

## Ethical note
This is an independent learning/practice project, not work completed for a paying client. Only publicly listed business contact information was considered; no private personal contact details are claimed.

## Suggested client use
A similar workflow can support prospect research, company list building, source logging, and lead-list quality checks. Final scope, target profile, fields, and verification standard should be agreed with the client first.
